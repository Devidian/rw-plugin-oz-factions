# OZ - Factions 0.5.0

- Leaders and officers can manage faction traders and market criers within faction capacity.
- Faction dissolution is blocked while a faction trader or market crier remains.
- Shop can debit and compensate faction trader payments through an idempotent faction account API.

Update Marketplace to 0.7.0 and Shop to 0.13.0 for faction NPCs. Keep existing faction and Wallet databases when updating. Remove faction NPCs and settle open trade before rolling back to older versions.

Requires Java 25 and Rising World PluginAPI 0.9.3.2.
